❤️ About Food Rescue

Food Rescue is a student business concept designed to reduce food waste while making meals more affordable for communities.

Every day, bakeries, restaurants and hotels may have food that remains unsold even though it is still suitable for consumption within the appropriate food-safety period.

Instead of allowing this surplus food to go to waste, Food Rescue provides a platform where businesses can offer it at discounted prices to people nearby.

The concept can create value for three groups: food businesses, customers and delivery partners.

🌱 Less food waste
💰 Additional revenue for businesses
🍱 Affordable food for customers
🚴 Employment opportunities for delivery partners
